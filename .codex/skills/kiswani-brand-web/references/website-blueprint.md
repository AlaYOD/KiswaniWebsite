## Experience goal

Create a premium, fast bilingual lighting catalog that helps homeowners, designers, contractors, and trade buyers move from inspiration to the correct product with minimal friction.

## Recommended information architecture

1. Home
2. Products
3. Category landing pages
4. Product detail pages
5. Inspiration or projects
6. About Kiswani
7. Contact and showroom information
8. Search

## Catalog hierarchy

- Interior Lighting
- Outdoor Lighting
- Bulb Lighting
- Technical Lighting
- Accessories
  - Drivers and Power Supplies
  - LED Films / Gardens
  - Emergency Lighting
  - Mosquito Killer
  - Pool Lighting
- Ventilators
- TCS Products
- Outlet / Sales

Treat Accessories as a parent category. Confirm the source wording “LED Films / Gardens” with the content owner before launch because it may be a translation or transcription issue.

## Homepage composition

1. Use an immersive lighting photograph or product scene in the hero.
2. Lead with a short brand promise and one primary catalog action.
3. Expose the four main lighting categories immediately after the hero.
4. Add a visual product-discovery section for room, application, or fixture type.
5. Feature selected products with clear specifications and restrained badges.
6. Use an inspiration or project strip to demonstrate installed lighting.
7. Finish with showroom, contact, and trade-support actions.

Use a dark architectural hero or a light editorial hero with concentrated yellow. Avoid a generic carousel as the primary experience.

## Component direction

- Header: horizontal bilingual lockup, compact navigation, search, language switcher, and a clear catalog action.
- Category card: large installed-product image, short label, thin icon or yellow rule, generous hit area.
- Product card: consistent image ratio, product name, category, essential technical facts, and restrained hover motion.
- Filters: category, application, installation type, color temperature, wattage, finish, dimensions, availability, and other confirmed catalog attributes.
- Product detail: image gallery, concise description, structured specifications, downloads, related products, and contact or quotation action.
- Footer: dark ink surface, white or yellow type, official lockup, contact details, category shortcuts, and social links.

## Layout and interaction

- Use a disciplined responsive grid and generous whitespace.
- Use rounded white overlay cards selectively, not on every component.
- Use subtle illumination transitions, line reveals, or warm light sweeps for motion.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep interactions useful rather than ornamental.
- Design touch targets for mobile and keep filters usable without horizontal overflow.
- Keep Arabic and English layouts visually equivalent while allowing natural text expansion and alignment.

## Content and CMS model

For every product, support:

- Name in Arabic and English
- Slug and category hierarchy
- Short and long description
- Primary and gallery images with alt text
- Model or SKU
- Dimensions and installation type
- Wattage, voltage, lumen output, color temperature, beam angle, CRI, IP rating, and finish when applicable
- Datasheet or manual download
- Availability or inquiry state
- Related products
- SEO title and description

Do not display empty specification rows. Confirm every technical field with the product source.

## Performance and SEO

- Use responsive images and modern formats without degrading fixture detail.
- Reserve image dimensions to prevent layout shift.
- Keep the first viewport lightweight and defer noncritical galleries.
- Give every route a unique title, description, canonical URL, and language alternate where applicable.
- Use clean category and product URLs and add structured data appropriate to products and organization details.
- Preserve crawlable text for headings and product facts; do not bake essential copy into images.

## Accessibility and QA

- Check contrast, focus visibility, keyboard navigation, form labels, and screen-reader names.
- Check Arabic shaping, RTL order, number formatting, punctuation, and mixed-direction content.
- Check logo clear space and aspect ratio at mobile, tablet, and desktop sizes.
- Check image crops so fixtures remain fully understandable.
- Test empty results, long names, missing images, unavailable products, and slow connections.
- Validate key flows: browse category, filter products, open a product, download data, switch language, search, and contact Kiswani.

## Avoid

- Filling most surfaces with yellow
- Low-contrast white text on yellow
- Generic neon gradients or cyberpunk lighting
- Excessive glass effects, shadows, or rounded cards
- Decorative icons that do not match the guide's line system
- Dense dashboards or ecommerce patterns that obscure the product imagery
- Rebuilding the logo, improvising a favicon from the monogram, or inventing unapproved lockups
