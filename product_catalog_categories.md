## Catalog Design Notes
To make this catalog "organized and easy to browse", it is recommended to follow these guidelines when designing it (whether as an electronic or printed catalog):
1. **Use Images:** Place a high-quality, descriptive image next to each main category.
2. **Hierarchical Structure:** Make "Accessories" a main category under which other types fall, to make it easier for the customer to find the desired product without complexity.
3. **Descriptive Icons:** Use simple icons next to each sub-category.
