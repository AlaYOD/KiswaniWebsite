# AI Rules

- Use an 8px spacing grid and the official Kiswani palette only: brand yellow `#FFDA01`, warm yellow `#FFA300`, ink `#0F1822`, warm black `#1E2722`, approved cool grays, and white.
- Do not introduce purple, blue, neon, or generic SaaS gradients. The Kiswani 2026 identity guideline is the authoritative color source.
- Reuse components and keep buttons, cards, inputs, tables, and modals consistent.
- Keep hierarchy strong, layouts uncluttered, and interactions accessible.
- Use Framer Motion for restrained transform and opacity animations.
- Respect reduced motion and optimize responsive images and rendering.
